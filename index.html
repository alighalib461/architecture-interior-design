\n Methodology\n

FROM FIRST IDEA
TO FINAL DETAIL

\n

\n A transparent, collaborative architectural journey ensuring precision at every milestone.\n

\n
\n\n
\n \n
\n 01\n

CONSULTATION

\n

\n Initial meetings to discuss ideas, budget, and project requirements. This phase establishes a strong foundation for the design process.\n

\n
\n\n \n
\n 02\n

DESIGN DEVELOPMENT

\n

\n Creation of concept sketches and 3D models based on client feedback. Iterative revisions ensure the design aligns with the client's vision.\n

\n
\n\n \n
\n 03\n

DOCUMENTATION

\n

\n Preparation of detailed architectural drawings, construction documentation, and material specifications for contractor bidding.\n

\n
\n\n \n
\n 04\n

CONSTRUCTION SUPERVISION

\n

\n On-site oversight and project coordination to ensure construction complies with architectural design, quality standards, and schedule.\n
